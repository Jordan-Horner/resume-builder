# Phoenix Wright Canon Research Addendum

Research date: 2026-08-19

Purpose: supplement the approved fictional career inventory with canon details
needed for a professionally structured legal-resume demonstration. This note
records factual claims and their sources; it is not resume copy.

## Source quality and boundaries

- Game transcripts are treated as the strongest available evidence because
  they reproduce localized in-game dialogue and case records.
- The Ace Attorney Wiki chronology is used for normalized years and office
  names. Its timeline warns that some dates are extrapolated from relative game
  statements, so year ranges are safer than unsupported day-level precision.
- No source reviewed names Phoenix Wright's real-world bar jurisdiction.
- The sources support a law degree but do not identify it as a J.D., LL.B., or
  another real-world credential.

## Education

1. Phoenix Wright attended Ivy University.
2. During Turnabout Memories he was an art student who was also planning to
   become a lawyer.
3. Later character history states that he studied art and law at Ivy University
   and received a law degree before joining Fey & Co. Law Offices.
4. The precise credential label and graduation date are not established well
   enough to map to a real-world J.D. or LL.B.

## Practice chronology and organization names

1. Wright began practicing under Mia Fey at Fey & Co. Law Offices in 2016.
2. After Mia Fey's death, he inherited the firm and operated it as Wright & Co.
   Law Offices from 2016 until his 2019 disbarment.
3. The office operated as the Wright Talent Agency during the disbarment era
   and became the Wright Anything Agency after Apollo Justice joined in 2026.
4. Wright presented forged evidence on April 19, 2019 and was subsequently
   disbarred.
5. Wright regained his attorney's badge on July 20, 2027.
6. The current game chronology supports these resume-safe year ranges:
   Fey & Co. Law Offices (2016), Wright & Co. Law Offices (2016-2019), Borscht
   Bowl Club / license interruption (2019-2027), and Wright Anything Agency
   after reinstatement (2027-2028 in the currently covered story timeline).

## Concrete legal work and case outcomes

### First murder trial

Wright served as lead defense attorney for Larry Butz in his first case. He
cross-examined the prosecution's witness, exposed the witness as the actual
killer, and obtained a not-guilty verdict.

Sources:

- https://aceattorney.fandom.com/wiki/The_First_Turnabout_-_Transcript
- https://aceattorney.fandom.com/wiki/Phoenix_Wright

### Miles Edgeworth and DL-6

Wright defended Miles Edgeworth against the Robert Hammond murder charge and
obtained a not-guilty verdict after establishing Yanni Yogi's responsibility.
When Edgeworth then confessed to killing his father, a separate DL-6 trial was
convened. Wright reconstructed the fifteen-year-old incident, used testimonial,
documentary, and physical evidence to identify Manfred von Karma as Gregory
Edgeworth's killer, and obtained a second not-guilty verdict for Edgeworth.

Sources:

- https://aceattorney.fandom.com/wiki/Turnabout_Goodbyes
- https://aceattorney.fandom.com/wiki/Turnabout_Goodbyes_-_Transcript_-_Part_6
- https://aceattorney.fandom.com/wiki/DL-6_Incident

### Ahlbi Ur'gaid in Khura'in

Wright volunteered to defend Ahlbi Ur'gaid in a court that had gone roughly
twenty years without a defense attorney. He challenged inconsistencies in the
Divination Seance, proved another person committed the crime, and obtained the
country's first not-guilty verdict since enactment of the Defense Culpability
Act twenty-three years earlier.

Sources:

## Resume-safe omissions

- Do not claim admission in California, Japan, or any named jurisdiction.
- Do not label the law degree as a J.D. or LL.B.
- Do not use exact graduation dates.
- Do not present wiki-extrapolated dates as indisputable day-level canon when a
  year range communicates the career chronology adequately.
- Do not count the civil Founder's Orb dispute as a defense victory for Wright;
  Phoenix appeared for the plaintiff and Apollo led the defense.
