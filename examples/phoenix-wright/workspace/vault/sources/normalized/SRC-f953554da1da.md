## Why this character works

Phoenix has enough career depth to exercise the Resume Builder without borrowing
the user's history:

- entry-level work under a named mentor;
- succession into responsibility for a small practice;
- repeated investigation, courtroom, and client-facing work;
- an ethically complicated professional failure and seven-year interruption;
- practice rebuilding and mentorship of two younger attorneys;
- reinstatement and a return to active courtroom work; and
- later work across two legal systems.

The resulting fixture can test chronology, adverse events, ethical wording,
career gaps, mentorship, role progression, evidence selection, and tailoring.

## Proposed career phases

### Phase 1 — Trainee defense attorney

**Approve in principle**

- Began courtroom practice under defense attorney Mia Fey at Fey & Co. Law
  Offices.
- Served as lead defense attorney in his first murder trial with Mia as
  co-counsel and obtained a not-guilty verdict for Larry Butz.
- Took responsibility for the office's active matters after Mia's death, first
  defending Maya Fey and then representing himself during the same matter.

Evidence: PW-SRC-004, PW-SRC-005.

### Phase 2 — Independent practice leader

**Approve in principle**

- Continued the practice as Wright & Co. Law Offices.
- Led investigations and courtroom defenses across the original trilogy,
  working with Maya Fey and other assistants and facing multiple prosecutors.
- Represented clients including an actor, a prosecutor, a police official, a
  spirit medium, a circus performer, and a former thief.
- Handled matters in which the objective was not simply an acquittal; in
  *Farewell, My Turnabout*, the client was culpable, making the episode useful
  for testing ethical nuance and outcome wording.

Evidence: PW-SRC-001, PW-SRC-004, PW-SRC-005.

### Phase 3 — Disbarment and professional interruption

**Include only with neutral, precise wording**

- Presented a forged diary page without knowing it was forged during a defense
  shortly after the original trilogy.
- Lost his license after the evidence was exposed and did not practice law for
  seven years.
- Adopted and raised Trucy Wright during this period.
- Worked at the Borscht Bowl Club as a pianist and poker player while continuing
  to investigate the circumstances behind the evidence and his disbarment.

Evidence: PW-SRC-005, PW-SRC-006.

Guardrail: never rewrite this as intentional evidence fabrication, never omit
the disbarment from a complete career history, and never turn an undefeated
poker description into a fabricated performance metric.

### Phase 4 — Practice rebuilding and mentorship

**Approve the broad phase; verify the exact authority claims**

- Brought Apollo Justice into the reorganized Wright Anything Agency as its
  practicing defense attorney.
- Guided Apollo's work and used the resolution of later cases to uncover the
  circumstances behind the forged evidence.
- Participated in organizing a simulated jurist-system trial connected to Vera
  Misham's defense and the investigation of the forgery.
- Met Athena Cykes while she was studying law in Europe and later recruited her
  to the agency.

Evidence: PW-SRC-002, PW-SRC-005, PW-SRC-006.

Guardrail: until checked against primary game material, describe Phoenix as
“participating in” or “organizing” the jurist-system trial—not as designing,
authorizing, or reforming the entire legal system. Do not credit him with cases
Apollo personally led.

### Phase 5 — Reinstated senior attorney

**Approve in principle**

- Regained his attorney's badge and returned to courtroom practice.
- Led *Turnabout Reclaimed*, identified as his first case after reinstatement.
- Practiced as the Wright Anything Agency's senior attorney while Apollo Justice
  and Athena Cykes handled their own matters.
- Took lead or supporting roles in the investigations and trials that concluded
  *Dual Destinies*.

Evidence: PW-SRC-002, PW-SRC-005, PW-SRC-006, PW-SRC-007.

Guardrail: mentorship can be claimed; blanket authorship of Apollo's or Athena's
case results cannot.

### Phase 6 — Cross-border defense work

**Approve in principle**

- Traveled to the Kingdom of Khura'in and entered a court system that had no
  active defense bar.
- Defended local guide Ahlbi Ur'gaid and later represented Maya Fey.
- Worked under the Defense Culpability Act, which exposed defense participants
  to their clients' punishment if the clients were convicted.
- Later supported Apollo as co-counsel in the final Khura'in matter.
- Continued domestic practice, including the special episode *Turnabout Time
  Traveler*.

Evidence: PW-SRC-002, PW-SRC-004, PW-SRC-005, PW-SRC-008.

Guardrail: do not claim Phoenix personally overthrew the government, repealed
the law, or single-handedly reformed Khura'in's legal system. The final outcome
was shared across several characters, with Apollo leading the decisive trial.

## Claims that must not appear

- “Won every case,” “never lost,” or a calculated win rate.
- “Exonerated every client.”
- “Founded” Fey & Co. or took credit for Mia Fey's work.
- “Managed” Apollo or Athena unless the exact fixture evidence supports formal
  supervisory authority; “mentored” is the safer current claim.
- Ownership of investigations, courtroom results, or reforms led by Apollo,
  Athena, Maya, Edgeworth, or other collaborators.
- Intentional use of forged evidence.
- Single-handed responsibility for ending the dark age of the law or changing
  Khura'in's government.
- Real-world claims about bar admission, jurisdictions, legal procedure, or
  professional qualifications.
- Any numeric caseload inferred by counting game episodes as a complete career.

## Proposed fixture direction

If approved, the first fixture should target a fictional **Senior Defense
Attorney / Small-Practice Mentor** profile. Its evidence themes would be:

- difficult criminal defense and investigative reasoning;
- client advocacy under pressure;
- practice continuity after the loss of a mentor;
- professional accountability and return after disbarment;
- mentoring attorneys without taking credit for their work; and
- adapting advocacy to an unfamiliar international court system.

The fixture should not imitate a conventional corporate operations resume, use
the user's employers or tools, or reuse the user's sentence structures. It
should sound like a legal-career document grounded only in the approved canon
inventory.
