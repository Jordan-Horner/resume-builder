# Defense of Miles Edgeworth and DL-6 reconstruction

Defended Miles Edgeworth against the Robert Hammond murder charge and obtained a not-guilty verdict after establishing Yanni Yogi's responsibility. In a separate DL-6 trial convened after Edgeworth confessed to killing his father, reconstructed the fifteen-year-old incident using testimonial, documentary, and physical evidence, identified Manfred von Karma as Gregory Edgeworth's killer, and obtained a second not-guilty verdict for Edgeworth.
