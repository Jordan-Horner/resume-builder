# Defense in a court without an active defense bar

Volunteered to represent local guide Ahlbi Ur'gaid in a Khura'inese court that had gone roughly twenty years without a defense attorney, challenged inconsistencies in the Divination Seance, proved another person committed the crime, and obtained the country's first not-guilty verdict since enactment of the Defense Culpability Act twenty-three years earlier.
