# Senior Defense Attorney

This fictional direction tests whether the engine can build a persuasive career
argument from a long, non-corporate history without turning every true vault
fact into resume content or taking credit from collaborators.

## Portable core

Current public-defense and senior trial-attorney sources consistently emphasize
courtroom advocacy, case investigation and preparation, evidence analysis,
clear written and oral communication, independent judgment, and collaboration
on complex matters. Active attorney status must be easy to find. Legal-career
guidance also favors relevant experience, precise action language, visible
dates, and selective detail over a complete case inventory.

## Fixture-specific overlay

Khura'in is useful fictional evidence of advocacy in an unfamiliar legal system,
but it is not a portable market requirement. The profile treats it as a
differentiating example of high-stakes trial work rather than as a claim that
real employers expect international practice.

## Research coverage and limits

The profile uses two current official attorney-role sources and two official law
school career offices. It is a deliberately narrow U.S.-oriented sample for a
fictional demonstration, not a survey of private criminal-defense hiring. The
market sources guide selection only; they do not establish anything Phoenix did.
